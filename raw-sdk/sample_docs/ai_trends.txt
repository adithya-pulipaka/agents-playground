AI Trends in 2025

Agentic AI systems have moved from research prototypes to production deployments. Companies are building fleets of specialized agents that collaborate on complex tasks rather than relying on a single general-purpose model.

Multimodal capabilities are now standard. Leading models can process text, images, audio, and video in a single context window, enabling use cases that were impossible just two years ago.

Inference costs have dropped dramatically. Running a capable model is now affordable for individual developers, which has fueled an explosion of personal and hobby AI projects.

Context windows have expanded to millions of tokens. This shifts the design challenge from "how do I fit my data into the context" to "how do I structure long contexts effectively."

On-device models are increasingly viable. Smaller, distilled models running locally on laptops and phones are enabling privacy-preserving applications that don't require sending data to external APIs.

The dominant challenge has shifted from model capability to reliability and evaluation — knowing whether your agent actually did what you asked it to do.
